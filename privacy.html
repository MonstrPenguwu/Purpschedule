Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

Stream Schedule is a personal tool for creating and sharing streaming schedules. This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers. All data (schedule settings, background images, credentials) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to third parties except as required to use connected services (TikTok, Discord) at your explicit request.

If you connect a TikTok account, your TikTok access token is stored locally in your browser only and is used solely to post content you explicitly initiate.
